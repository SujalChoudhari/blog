I have started the second pass on Samhara's Daughter after completing a pass on the first five chapters. Samhara's Daughter is the project name; When the Gods Went Silent is the book title. I am back in the manuscript with those five chapters behind me and the rest still to work through.

I hope to finish the manuscript before September. For now, I am taking the work section by section and making the revisions that are actually needed.

Kevat's story is separate in my mind. It deserves a book of its own, but I do not know when I will have time to write it. I am leaving the timing unknown instead of trying to squeeze it into the current book.

There is a third idea as well, but it would be a slow burn. I am not trying to turn it into another active project yet.
