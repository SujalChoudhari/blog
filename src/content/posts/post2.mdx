# My Hacktoberfest 2023 Journey

I was thrilled to take part in Hacktoberfest 2023. Participating helped me grow as a developer and contribute to open-source projects.

## My Contributions

During Hacktoberfest 2023, my contribution was centered on two of my own repositories:

- [Coda (code) - A Programming Language written in C++](https://lnkd.in/gmQw4xZJ)
- [CodaWiki (low-code) - Wiki for the project Coda](https://lnkd.in/geqTs9nG)

Working on these projects challenged my coding skills. Coda, a programming language written in C++, pushed me to explore new programming concepts. CodaWiki, the low-code wiki for the Coda project, let me focus on documentation and user engagement. I wanted the project to be accessible and useful to a broader audience.

## My Milestone

I remember that a tree was planted in my name as part of that contribution. You can view my certificate [here](https://lnkd.in/gBRcegir).

## Join Me

Hacktoberfest 2023 ran until October 31st. Developers of all skill levels could contribute to open-source projects and learn from each other.

I invite you to join me. Whether you are experienced or just starting, Hacktoberfest is a good place to collaborate and learn. By contributing to my repositories, you can improve your skills and contribute to the open-source community.

Participating in Hacktoberfest was rewarding, and I am grateful for the support and camaraderie of the community. It reminded me what collaboration can do when people work toward the same goal.

Thank you for being part of this community. I hope to see you at Hacktoberfest.
