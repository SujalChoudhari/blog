# A Remarkable Victory at Smart India Hackathon 2023 (SIH23)

## The Journey Begins

Our team won the Smart India Hackathon 2023 (SIH23). I was proud to be part of the "Code Voyagers" team from Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering. We worked hard and stayed committed throughout the competition.

## The Team Effort

Participating in SIH23 as a second-year (SY) team seemed like an impossible feat. However, our team (Saniyaa B Shetty, Soham Patil, Anish Shetty, Anish Sharma, and Ayush Upadhyay) dedicated countless hours to research and development. The enthusiasm and determination of each team member helped us compete and win at this level.

## The Experience

The competition took place at St. Joseph's College of Engineering, our nodal center. We felt welcomed and supported throughout the event. Meeting Sir Nitin Bidhe was one of the highlights of our time on campus. We also interacted with Prime Minister Narendra Modi, which gave the team another boost during the competition.

## Gratitude

Thank you to Abhay Jere and AICTE for organizing the event. SIH23 gave us a chance to compete, collaborate, and show what we could build.

## The Victory

Winning SIH23 was an important milestone for me as a developer. I am proud of what we achieved together, and the experience showed me how much teamwork and perseverance matter.

Thank you to everyone who supported us on this journey.
