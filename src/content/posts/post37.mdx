### Five years of focus and growth

From 2020 to the end of 2025, I spent most of my time learning, building, and competing. These are the main points I remember from those years.

In 2020, a setback pushed me to work harder. I focused on coding, game development, and creative projects, often losing track of time.

![The Start](/images/posts/37a.jpg)

For two years, I explored game engines and programming. I spent months experimenting with shaders and simulations and learning as I went.

In 2022, I started college and focused on building projects and challenging myself. I also created my own programming language in C++. It was exciting to see those ideas take shape.

![The Machine](/images/posts/37b.jpg)

December 2023 brought my first big win at a national hackathon. After that, some weekends ended with a win and others ended with a new lesson. Teams changed, projects shifted, and some things did not go as planned. I learned to adapt and keep working.

![Competition](/images/posts/37c.jpg)

I wrote books, published software, and kept learning. In the summer of 2025, I interned at a fintech startup and built real-time monitoring systems. Seeing that work used was rewarding.

![Wins](/images/posts/37d.jpg)

Placement season brought new opportunities and decisions. Some worked out and some did not. I learned from each experience.

By the end of 2025, I was leading an AI project at a startup accelerator. I was proud of the progress.

![Placement](/images/posts/37e.jpg)

---

### Balance and connection in 2026

In 2026, I am paying more attention to balance. I still enjoy building, but I also make room for friends, family, and life outside my screen.

![The Cost](/images/posts/37f.jpg)

For years, I focused on solving problems and reaching goals. Now I am learning to set healthier boundaries, say yes to new experiences, and appreciate the simple parts of the day.

I am proud of what I achieved. In 2026, balance matters to me as much as the work.
