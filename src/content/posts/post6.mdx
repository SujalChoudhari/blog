# Team Entropy and the CLEO Buildathon Project

## Team Entropy and the CLEO project

Our team, Entropy, was recognized for the work we presented in the LLM and Diffusion category at the Buildathon, as I remember it. I attended an event hosted by YourStory Media and Ola Krutrim in collaboration with 100xEngineers. The project repository describes CLEO as the "100x Engineers x YourStory Buildathon Winning Project" and names it "CLEO - AI-Powered Sales Assistant." Entropy is the team name, while CLEO is the project name.

### My memory of the event

I remember an award ceremony at DevSparks 24. Varun Mayya presented the prize, and I had the chance to present the work to the audience there. Receiving the award and showing the work in person made the event special for me.

### People I met at DevSparks 24

During DevSparks 24, I met Sridev Ramesh and Siddhant Goswami. I appreciated the chance to speak with them and to thank the organizers and participants who made the event possible.

### Special thanks to the team

Special thanks to Soham Patil, Surabhi Waingankar, and Srinath Reddy for their support and collaboration. We worked hard together, and I am glad we got to share the result.
