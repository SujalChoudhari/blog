# Winning the LLM & Diffusion Category at the Buildathon

## Team Entropy won the LLM and Diffusion category

Our team, Entropy, won the LLM and Diffusion category at the Buildathon. I attended an event hosted by YourStory Media and Ola Krutrim in collaboration with 100xEngineers. The official result names Team Entropy as the winner and identifies Cleo as an AI sales assistant project. Entropy is the team name, while Cleo is the project name.

### The award ceremony

The award ceremony took place at DevSparks 24. Varun Mayya presented the prize, and I had the chance to present the work to the audience there. Receiving the award and showing the work in person made the event special for me.

### People I met at DevSparks 24

During DevSparks 24, I met Sridev Ramesh and Siddhant Goswami. I appreciated the chance to speak with them and to thank the organizers and participants who made the event possible.

### Special thanks to the team

Special thanks to Soham Patil, Surabhi Waingankar, and Srinath Reddy for their support and collaboration. We worked hard together, and I am glad we got to share the result.
