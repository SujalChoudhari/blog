# Coding Mastery: Rendering Realities

## About the book

I wrote **"Coding Mastery: Rendering Realities"** for readers who want to understand how graphics and game visuals are built through code. The book covers rendering engines, mathematical foundations, geometry, lighting, shading, curves, surfaces, fractals, procedural generation, animation, and interactivity.

## What it covers

- **Foundational concepts**: The building blocks and algorithms behind rendering engines.
- **Mathematics and geometry**: The transformations used to position and view objects in virtual spaces.
- **Lighting and shading**: Techniques for giving scenes depth and detail.
- **Advanced topics**: Curves, surfaces, fractals, procedural generation, animation, and interactivity.

## Who should read it

The book is aimed at students, aspiring game developers, and programmers who want to learn more about computer graphics. It combines theory with practical ideas for understanding rendering and related topics.
